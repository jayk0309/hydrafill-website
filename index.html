Frequently Asked Questions

Is HydraFill a medical device? +
No, HydraFill is a wellness tracking app, not a medical device. It's designed for personal hydration awareness. Always consult a healthcare provider for medical advice about your hydration needs.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes! You can cancel anytime from your device's app store subscription settings. Your subscription will continue until the end of the current billing period.
How is my data stored? +
Your data is encrypted and stored securely in our Supabase backend. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Do you have Android support? +
Yes! HydraFill is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Download whichever matches your device.
Can I sync between devices? +
Yes, sign in with the same account on any device and your data syncs across all your devices.
