Privacy Policy

HydraFill — Hydration Tracking App

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HydraFill ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application and website.

This Privacy Policy explains our information practices, including what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information. By using HydraFill, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use HydraFill.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication Data

When you create a HydraFill account, we collect:

  • Email address — for account creation, login, and password recovery
  • Password — securely hashed and stored (never in plaintext)
  • Full name — optional, for personalization
  • Body type preference — male/female for body visualization
  • Daily hydration goal — based on your inputs during onboarding
  • Account creation date — automatically recorded

Third-party auth: If you sign in via Google or Apple, we receive your email and name from them. We do NOT access your contacts, location, or other Google/Apple account data.

2.2 Hydration & Health Data

You voluntarily provide us with:

  • Drink logs — time, type (water, coffee, tea, juice, etc.), volume, and custom notes
  • Custom drinks — name, category, volume, calories (optional), personal notes
  • Streak data — daily goal completion history and achievement milestones
  • Analytics insights — weekly/monthly hydration trends and patterns

This data is non-medical and for wellness tracking only. HydraFill is not a medical device and should not replace professional medical advice.

2.3 Payment & Subscription Data

HydraFill uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When you purchase a subscription:

  • RevenueCat receives: Your app purchase information, subscription status, and renewal dates
  • Apple/Google receive: Your payment method (we never see your credit card details)
  • We receive: Your subscription tier (free/premium), expiration date, and entitlements

Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never store, process, or see your credit card numbers.

2.4 Photos & Media

If you upload a profile avatar:

  • Photos are stored in Supabase Storage (encrypted at rest)
  • You can delete your photo anytime from Profile settings
  • We never share photos with third parties

2.5 Push Notifications

If you enable reminders, we collect:

  • Notification preferences — times you want to be reminded
  • Device tokens — to send notifications to your device

You can disable notifications anytime in app settings or your device settings.

2.6 Device & Usage Data

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type & OS version — iOS/Android and app version
  • App usage patterns — features you use and session duration
  • Crash reports — if the app crashes, we receive anonymous error logs
  • IP address — for security and fraud detection

2.7 Location Data

HydraFill does NOT request or collect your location. We do not know where you are unless you explicitly tell us (e.g., in profile notes).

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data sent to/from HydraFill uses HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
  • Encryption at rest: Supabase encrypts your data at rest on their servers
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed with a strong one-way algorithm, never stored in plaintext
  • Access controls: Only authorized HydraFill team members can access user data
  • Regular reviews: We monitor our backend's security and compliance status regularly

Important: No security system is 100% impenetrable. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact us at support@hydrafill.fit.

7. Your Privacy Rights

If you're in the European Union, United Kingdom, California, or another region with privacy laws, you have rights regarding your data:

7.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of all data we hold about you. Contact us at support@hydrafill.fit and we'll provide your data within 30 days.

7.2 Right to Correction

You can update your account information (name, email, goal, body type) directly in the app under Profile settings.

7.3 Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data. We'll delete everything within 30 days, except where legally required to retain it (e.g., billing records).

7.4 Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a portable format (JSON/CSV). Contact support@hydrafill.fit.

7.5 Right to Object

You can opt out of analytics or push notifications in app settings.

7.6 Right to Restrict Processing

You can request that we limit how we use your data. Contact support@hydrafill.fit.

7.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

If we're processing data based on your consent (like analytics), you can withdraw that consent anytime in settings.

How to Exercise Your Rights: Email us at support@hydrafill.fit with your request and we'll respond within 30 days (up to 45 days for GDPR where permitted).

For users ages 13-18, parental consent is recommended for account creation and data collection.
